About
Moth Lite v1.0
A field forecasting tool for Wyoming & Colorado moths. Data sources: iNaturalist, GBIF, Open-Meteo, EPA Ecoregions, OpenStreetMap.
iNaturalist
Moth Lite is a forecasting and field reference tool. Please document your moth sightings on iNaturalist to contribute to citizen science.
Legal
Terms of Use
Moth Lite is provided for informational and field planning purposes only. Species likelihood scores are heuristic estimates and are not verified biological forecasts. We are not liable for any misidentification of species. Data is sourced from third-party providers (iNaturalist, GBIF, Open-Meteo, EPA, OpenStreetMap) and is subject to their respective terms. Use of this app constitutes acceptance of these terms.
Privacy Policy
Moth Lite does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. Your GPS location is used only on-device to fetch forecasts and species data from third-party APIs (iNaturalist, GBIF, Open-Meteo, Nominatim). Sightings you log are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded. No analytics, no account required.
Location Permission
Moth Lite requests location access to automatically detect your GPS position for weather forecasts, ecoregion detection, and nearby species queries. Location is sent directly to iNaturalist, GBIF, Open-Meteo, and Nominatim APIs. It is never stored on any Moth Lite server.
